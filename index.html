Klaayo BINGO Rules

Your Board: Choose between a 5×5 (1 to 25), 6×6 (1 to 36), or 7×7 (1 to 49) grid arranged in your secret sequence.

How Turns Work:

• Players take turns picking one uncalled number from their grid.

• When a number is picked, every player crosses it out on their own board!

• The circular scribble is drawn in the calling player's unique pen color.

Secret Lines & B-I-N-G-O:

• When numbers align across any of YOUR rows, columns, or diagonals, a thin laser slash cuts across your board.

• Strict Privacy: Opponents cannot see your completed lines or your B-I-N-G-O progress during the match, and you cannot see theirs!

• Post-Match Card Reveal: When the match concludes, you can inspect the winner's and all opponents' secret cards!

• 1 Line = B, 2 Lines = I, 3 Lines = N, 4 Lines = G, 5 Lines = O.

Winning: The first player to reach 5 completed lines shouts BINGO and wins the match!
